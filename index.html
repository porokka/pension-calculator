What this model assumes
  • Today's rules for the whole career. Each working year uses the current contribution and accrual rates of the country you were in. Past rates were different, and Estonia's pillar II only started in 2002.
  • After a move. Each country pays its own share from its own retirement age, as EU rules require. The Finnish part keeps being revalued until it starts. Estonia's pillar I base amount is paid in proportion to the years worked in Estonia, a simplification of the EU pro-rata rule.
  • Finland. TyEL contribution 24.4 % of gross (you 7.30 %, the employer the rest). Accrual 1.5 % of each year's earnings, revalued 80 % by wages. The life expectancy coefficient and the retirement ages for people born after 1967 are approximations of ETK forecasts, not official values. The national pension top-up for low earners is left out.
  • Ministry of Finance proposal. Published in October 2026: the lowest retirement age rises half a year per birth cohort until 70, reached by those born in 1976. It is a civil-service proposal, not law. Ages for later cohorts are an extrapolation.
  • Estonia pillar I. Base 399.24 € plus 10.477 € per year of service (values from April 2026). Each year counts half as service and half by your wage relative to the average; in pillar II the wage half is reduced by a fifth. Both values are indexed here at 80 % of real wage growth. Retirement age after 65 rises about one month per birth year, an estimate.
  • Estonia pillars II and III. Pillar II gets your 2, 4 or 6 % plus 4 % from social tax. Pillar III contributions earn a 22 % income tax refund on up to 15 % of gross income, at most 6 000 € a year.
  • The invested row. Finland: the full 24.4 %. Estonia: the 20 % pension part of social tax, plus your pillar II and III contributions, without the tax refund. This is a thought experiment. Pay-as-you-go contributions fund today's pensioners and cannot be redirected.
  • Not included. Income tax on pensions, market crashes near retirement, living past the payout age (state pensions keep paying, a spent pot does not), and the disability and survivor cover the statutory systems include.
An estimate for comparison, not financial advice and not an official pension forecast. For your real accrual, check your pension record at tyoelake.fi and your account at pensionikeskus.ee.
